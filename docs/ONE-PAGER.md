# Peggy

### Peer-reviewed Evidence Gathering, Grounding & Yielding Knowledge

Your research assistant, from research idea to final results.

Peggy helps researchers assess whether an idea is worth pursuing, design a stronger study, manage the research process and evaluate findings against existing literature — all in one place.

## The problem

Research is fragmented, time-consuming and difficult to validate.

- Too much time spent searching: Researchers spend weeks reviewing literature before knowing whether a meaningful research gap exists.
- Inconsistent evidence: Studies measure and report similar questions differently, making findings difficult to compare.
- Disconnected workflows: Objectives, study design, ethics, budgets and results live in separate documents.
- Unreliable AI: General AI tools produce convincing answers without reliably distinguishing peer-reviewed research from preprints, unverified sources or retracted papers.

## The solution: One research workspace, grounded in evidence.

| Capability         | What Peggy does                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Idea validation    | Identifies potential research gaps by scanning titles and abstracts, checking aims and objectives against project literature, and showing how many papers support each gap. |
| Evidence integrity | Labels sources as peer-reviewed, preprint or unverified. Excludes retracted papers and links every claim to its supporting passage.                                         |
| Study design       | Generates evidence-informed checklists for sampling, methodology, analysis and ethics. Drafts proposals and links budgets to research objectives.                           |
| Results comparison | Compares findings against published literature and identifies differences in methodology, including covariates other studies adjusted for.                                  |
| Project management | Tracks objective status and history, flagging connected work affected by changes to research objectives.                                                                    |
| Data profiling     | Profiles uploaded datasets directly in the browser. Researchers classify administrative and analytical columns while raw participant-level data stays on their device.      |
| Research assistant | Answers questions using traceable sources, communicates limitations and requires researcher approval before implementing suggested changes.                                 |

## Built around research integrity and privacy

- Project isolation: Each research project remains separate.
- Responsible AI: Uses AI providers whose terms prohibit training on user content.
- Data privacy: Participant-level data is never stored on Peggy's servers. Only researcher-approved summaries are shared.
- Transparency: Answers show the sources and evidence used.
- Data control: Papers, projects and accounts can be deleted, with associated data removed.

## Who benefits?

For researchers

- Faster, better-informed research starts.
- Stronger proposals and fewer surprises during ethics review and peer review.
- Traceable evidence and a single workspace throughout the research lifecycle.

For universities and research institutions

- More consistent proposals and protocols.
- Structured evidence that makes studies easier to compare.
- Auditable research workflows and privacy controls for institutional oversight.

## What Peggy is not

Peggy is not clinical advice, a full systematic review or a replacement for supervisors, statisticians or ethics committees. It supports research decisions; researchers remain responsible for them. Institutions should assess its use against their own data governance and approval requirements.
