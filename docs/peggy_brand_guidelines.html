Logo usage

Do
  • Use on #f2f4ef, #f8faf6, #ffffff, or #2d4a42
  • Maintain clear space equal to the mark height on all sides
  • Use wordmark and mark together at small sizes
  • Use mark alone as app icon / favicon
Don't
  • Recolor the mark outside the palette
  • Stretch or distort the lockup
  • Place on low-contrast or busy backgrounds
  • Use the wordmark without the mark at icon sizes
